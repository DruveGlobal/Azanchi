Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 November 2026

1. Who we are

Druve Technology Ltd, a company registered in Nigeria, trading as Azanchi ("Azanchi", "we", "us"), is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy, for the Azanchi desktop application, website and related services (the "Service"). Registered address: 60a Oshogbo Street, Surulere, Lagos, Nigeria. Data Protection Officer: privacy@azanchi.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal data we process about:

  • Users — people who create an Azanchi account and use the Service directly;
  • Meeting participants — other people who appear in recordings and transcripts made by an Azanchi user, who themselves may not hold an Azanchi account. They have the same statutory data-protection rights as any other person; because Azanchi processes their data on the account holder's instruction, those rights are exercised through, or with, the account holder. Section 9 explains how that works.

3. What data we collect

From you directly

  • Account data: name, email address, and authentication data when you sign in via Google or Microsoft (we receive limited profile information from these providers per their consent screens — we do not receive your Google/Microsoft password).
  • Payment data: billing name, plan selection, and transaction history. Full card/bank details are collected and processed directly by our payment processors; we receive only confirmation of payment status and limited billing metadata, not full card numbers.
  • Support communications: anything you send us via email or support channels.

Generated through your use of the Service

  • Meeting audio captured on the user's device during recording sessions the user starts. Audio is processed on-device and not uploaded; Transcripts are produced from that audio on the user's device.
  • Transcripts generated from that audio.
  • AI-generated notes, summaries, action items, and draft follow-up content derived from transcripts.
  • Calendar data, if the user connects Google Calendar or Microsoft 365 — meeting titles, times, and attendee lists, used to detect meetings and prepare briefs. Calendar metadata is retained while the integration is connected and deleted when the user disconnects it.
  • Usage/device data: app version, operating system, crash logs, and feature-usage analytics, collected to maintain and improve the Service.
  • Voice and speaker data: speaker labels are generated for each meeting to attribute turns within that meeting. We do not create or store voiceprints, and we do not use voice for biometric identification or to link a person across meetings.

From meeting participants (who may not be Azanchi users)

When an Azanchi user records a meeting, we may process the voice, likeness (if video is captured), and spoken content of other participants in that meeting, even though they haven't created an Azanchi account or agreed to this policy directly. See Section 7 for how we handle this.

4. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

  • Provide the core Service (meeting detection, recording, transcription, note generation, calendar sync);
  • Process payments and manage your subscription;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Maintain security, detect fraud/abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • Improve and debug the Service (using aggregated or de-identified data where feasible);
  • Send you Service-related communications (billing notices, security alerts, material policy changes); with your separate opt-in consent, product updates or marketing.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use meeting content to train, fine-tune or improve any model by default. We may operate a separate research programme in which customers may choose to contribute content; that programme requires its own opt-in, given separately and not bundled with acceptance of these terms, states what the content would be used for and which model it would improve, does not affect the Service or its price if declined, and can be withdrawn at any time for future use. Absent that specific opt-in, customer content is used only to provide the Service to the account that created it, to secure the Service, and to comply with law.

5. Legal basis for processing (NDPA / GDPR-style basis)

Depending on the processing activity, our legal basis includes:

  • Consent — for connecting third-party integrations and for optional marketing or research communications, given separately and withdrawable at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — for account creation, billing, and providing the core Service you've signed up for;
  • Legitimate interest — for security, fraud prevention, and Service improvement, balanced against your rights;
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with law (e.g., tax/financial recordkeeping for payments).
  • Legitimate interests — for processing meeting-participant data at the instruction of the account holder who records their own business meeting, balanced against participants' rights. We have completed a balancing assessment and can provide a summary on request. The account holder's own consent and disclosure obligations are set out in Section 5 of our Terms of Service.

7. A note on meeting participants who are not Azanchi users

When you use Azanchi to record a meeting, other participants' voices, words, and (if video is captured) likeness become part of the data we process — even though they never created an account or agreed to this policy. This is why Section 5 of our Terms of Service makes you, the user initiating the recording, responsible for obtaining any consent required by law before recording. From our side:

  • Azanchi acts as a processor for meeting content — audio, transcripts and notes generated from them — processing it on the instruction of the account holder, who is the controller of that meeting record. Azanchi acts as a controller for account, billing, support and product-telemetry data. Where we act as a processor, our processing is governed by our Data Processing Addendum, which forms part of the agreement with Business customers.
  • We retain it under the same retention rules as your other meeting data (Section 8).
  • A non-user participant who wants to exercise a data-subject right (e.g., request deletion of their voice data from a specific recording) can contact us at privacy@azanchi.com, and we'll work with the Azanchi user who made the recording to address the request, consistent with our obligations under applicable law.

8. Data retention

  • Free plan: meeting notes and transcripts are retained while the account is active. If an account is inactive for 90 consecutive days, we will email the account holder and, absent a response within 30 days, delete the meeting content. Account and billing data are retained as described below.
  • Pro and Business plans: meeting content is retained for the duration of the active subscription and for 30 days after cancellation, to allow export and reinstatement, after which it is deleted.
  • Account data: retained while the account is active and deleted within 30 days of closure, except that billing and transaction records are retained for 7 years to meet Nigerian tax and financial-record obligations, and limited records are retained where needed to resolve a dispute or comply with law.
  • Meeting audio: audio is captured and transcribed on the user's own device and is never transmitted to Azanchi or to any third party. See Section 3 (What data we collect). Deleting a meeting in the app deletes the local audio file; uninstalling the app removes all local audio.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Delete your data ("right to erasure");
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (e.g., disconnecting a calendar integration, or deleting recorded meetings);
  • Port your data to another service in a structured format;
  • Object to certain processing, including for marketing;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Nigeria: the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). In Ghana: the Data Protection Commission. In Kenya: the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@azanchi.com. We will acknowledge a rights request within 7 days and respond substantively within 30 days. If we need longer because a request is complex, we will tell you within that period and give a revised date. Data is provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (JSON or Markdown).

10. International data transfers

Meeting audio stays on the user's device and is not transferred. Account, billing, support and transcript-derived data may be processed outside Nigeria by the providers listed in Section 6 (sub-processors). Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on a lawful transfer mechanism recognised under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where applicable, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012, including contractual safeguards and, where required, an assessment of the destination's legal protections. Customers on the Business plan may request a summary of the safeguards that apply to their data.

11. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure credential storage (session credentials are stored in your device's OS keychain rather than in plain text). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because meeting audio is processed on the user's device and never transmitted, it is outside the scope of any server-side breach. We apply encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access controls, session credentials held in the device's OS keychain, and access logging. We maintain a documented breach response process, and where a breach is likely to result in a risk to data subjects we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware, notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya within 72 hours where Kenyan data subjects are affected, and notify affected users without undue delay.

12. Cookies and website analytics

Our website does not use cookies for advertising or profiling. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless measurement service that records aggregate page views and performance data without setting cookies or collecting personal identifiers. The desktop application sends limited product telemetry — app version, operating system, crash reports and feature events — to help us maintain the Service, as described in Section 3. If we introduce cookies or a consent-requiring analytics or advertising service, we will update this policy, publish a cookie table and request consent where required before the change takes effect.

13. Children

The Service is a business tool, is not directed at anyone under 18, and is not intended for personal, family or household use. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if we learn that we have, we will delete it. See Section 15 of our Terms of Service.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new version number and date, and we will keep prior versions available on request. For material changes — including any change to how we handle meeting content, retention, sub-processors, or model training — we will give at least 30 days' notice by email and in-app before the change takes effect.
